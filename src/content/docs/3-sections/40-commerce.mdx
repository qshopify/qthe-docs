## Featured product

Sell one product anywhere: gallery, price, variant picker, buy buttons, Shop Pay Installments and accelerated checkout. Images, videos and 3D models all play here. Add **Custom liquid** or app blocks under the form.

<SettingsTable section="featured-product" />

## Featured collection

A grid or a carousel of products from one collection. **Card style** switches between the standard card and a compact card.

<img src="/images/docs/featured-collection.png" alt="The Featured collection section: a heading and a row of products from one collection." width="1425" height="814" />

<SettingsTable section="featured-collection" />

## Collection tabs

Several collections behind tabs — *Bestsellers*, *New*, *Gifts* — so shoppers can browse without leaving the page. Add one **Tab** block per collection.

<img src="/images/docs/collection-tabs.png" alt="The Collection tabs section: mood chips that switch which collection's products show." width="1425" height="797" />

<SettingsTable section="collection-tabs" />

## Collection list

Collections as image tiles or circles. Without an image, a collection shows its first product's image.

<img src="/images/docs/collection-list.png" alt="The Collection list section: a grid of collection tiles." width="1425" height="739" />

<SettingsTable section="collection-list" />

## Shoppable image

A lifestyle photo with hotspots. Each **Hotspot** block links a product to a point on the image; set its **Horizontal position** and **Vertical position** in percent.

<img src="/images/docs/shoppable-image.png" alt="The Shoppable image section: a room photo with hotspot dots that open a product card." width="1425" height="848" />

<SettingsTable section="shoppable-image" />

## Bundle builder

Shoppers fill a set from a collection and add it to the cart in one step. The **Bundle discount** setting is display-only: create a matching automatic discount in **Discounts** so the price actually changes at checkout.

<img src="/images/docs/bundle-builder.png" alt="The Bundle builder section: pick products to assemble a set, with a running total." width="1425" height="717" />

<SettingsTable section="bundle-builder" />

## Gift card purchase

Sell your gift card from any page, with amount buttons and the option to send it to a recipient by email on a chosen date.

<img src="/images/docs/gift-card-purchase.png" alt="The Gift card purchase section: denomination picker and a recipient form." width="1425" height="600" />

<SettingsTable section="gift-card-purchase" />

Products the shopper looked at recently, stored in their own browser. Choose a fallback **Collection** for first-time visitors.
