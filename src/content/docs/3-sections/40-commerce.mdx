## Note wall

Every product of a collection drawn as its [note strip](/docs/products/note-strip), like perfumer's blotters pinned to a board. Choose a **Collection** and how many **Products to show**, then a **Layout**:

- **Columns** — vertical strips side by side with the product names below. It works best as the first section on the home page.
- **Rows** — a ledger with one product per row, its strip read from left to right, with price and quick add.

**Show family filters** adds a row of family chips that filter the wall. The families come from the `family` field of the products' **Note** entries. Under **Text**, add an eyebrow, heading, intro, a button and a link; set **Heading level** to **H1** when the wall is the first section on the page.

## Featured product

Sell one product anywhere: gallery, price, variant picker, buy buttons, Shop Pay Installments and accelerated checkout. Images, videos and 3D models all play here. Add **Custom liquid** or app blocks under the form.

The **Tier** layout reuses the top tier of the product page's **Note tiers** layout: the product's note strip as a rail on the left, a large title, its top, heart and base notes, the buy button and one image. It's the layout for new Featured product sections.

## Collection tabs

Several collections behind tabs — *Bestsellers*, *New*, *Gifts* — so shoppers can browse without leaving the page. Add one **Tab** block per collection.

Each tab has a **Color**, used for the active tab's underline in the stacked layout and for a small tick in the split layout. Leave it blank to use the tab collection's `custom.color` metafield, then the family color that matches the tab's label, or else the color scheme's strip colors in turn.

## Collection list

Collections as image tiles or circles. Without an image, a collection shows its first product's image.

The **Bands** style shows each collection as a tall band in its family color, as tall as its product count compared with the largest collection. A band's color is the block's **Color**; when that's blank, it's the collection's own `custom.color` metafield, then the family color that matches the collection's title, or else the color scheme's strip colors in turn. Bands is the style for new Collection list sections.

## Shoppable image

A lifestyle photo with hotspots. Each **Hotspot** block links a product to a point on the image; set its **Horizontal position** and **Vertical position** in percent.

With **Product card** set to **In-this-room list, cards over image**, a list beside the image shows every hotspot product with its note strip and price, and an *Add all* button; choosing a hotspot opens its card over the image. **Hotspot marker** draws each hotspot as a short **Note strip of the product** or as a **Dot**.

## Bundle builder

Shoppers fill a set from a collection and add it to the cart in one step. The **Bundle discount** setting is display-only: create a matching automatic discount in **Discounts** so the price actually changes at checkout.

Choose a **Style**: **Strips** shows tall slots that fill with each pick's note strip, beside the products to choose from, each with its strip down the edge. **Cards** shows product cards beside a summary panel.

## Gift card purchase

Sell your gift card from any page, with amount buttons and the option to send it to a recipient by email on a chosen date.

**Show note strip** draws a strip down the gift card's edge. Pick a **Strip product** to use its notes; without one, the strip uses the card's color scheme strip colors.

<img src="/images/docs/gift-card-purchase.png" alt="The Gift card purchase section: denomination picker and a recipient form." width="1425" height="600" />

## Recently viewed

Products the shopper looked at recently, stored in their own browser. Choose a fallback **Collection** for first-time visitors.

**Card style** sets how each product shows. **Line** is a single scrolling line of small thumbnails, each with a thin note strip under it. It's the style for new Recently viewed sections. The other styles match the product recommendations cards or show a compact strip of image, title and price.

## Blog posts

Your latest articles, or articles you pick, as cards.

Choose a **Layout**: **Lead article and list** shows the newest article large, with the next ones listed under a heavy rule. **Cards in a row** shows each article as a card, swiping on phones.
