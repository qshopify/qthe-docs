These sections tell your brand story. Most of them share the same heading controls — **Eyebrow**, **Heading**, **Intro**, **Heading alignment** and an optional link — so a page reads consistently.

## Rich text

Copy-led sections built from **Eyebrow**, **Heading**, **Text** and **Button** blocks. The **Split** layout puts the heading on the left and flows the text into columns.

<img src="/images/docs/rich-text.png" alt="A Rich text section: eyebrow, heading, body copy and a button." width="1425" height="510" />

<SettingsTable section="rich-text" />

## Image with text

An image beside your copy, or a card that overlaps the image. Add a **List** block for ingredients, steps or benefits.

<img src="/images/docs/image-with-text.png" alt="An Image with text section: photo beside a column of copy and a list block." width="1425" height="1108" />

<SettingsTable section="image-with-text" />

## Multicolumn

One section, several styles: icon columns, numbered steps, cards, a spec band, a timeline, or offers with prices. Each **Column** block is one item.

<img src="/images/docs/multicolumn.png" alt="A Multicolumn section in the icon-columns style." width="1425" height="316" />

<SettingsTable section="multicolumn" />

## Collage

A grid of images, products and collections with one large item. **Darken image for light text** is on by default so the overlaid copy stays readable on any photo — turn it off per item if your photo's already dark enough on its own.

<img src="/images/docs/collage.png" alt="The Collage grid layout: one large item beside two stacked items, each with a label and title overlaid at the bottom." width="1441" height="776" />

<SettingsTable section="collage" />

## Testimonials

Customer quotes as cards or one large quote at a time. Link a quote to the product it's about.

<img src="/images/docs/testimonials.png" alt="The Testimonials section: one quote at a time with a star rating and pager." width="1425" height="509" />

<SettingsTable section="testimonials" />

<Callout type="warning" title="Use real reviews only">
Quotes should come from real customers. Shopify and consumer-protection laws don't allow invented reviews.
</Callout>

## Logo list

Press logos or partner marks, with an optional press quote. A logo can link to the article.

<img src="/images/docs/logo-list.png" alt="The Logo list section: a row of press or partner logos." width="1425" height="317" />

<SettingsTable section="logo-list" />

## Comparison table

Compare your product with up to three alternatives, row by row. **Tint column 1** highlights your column.

<img src="/images/docs/comparison.png" alt="The Comparison section: a table with your column highlighted against alternatives." width="1425" height="683" />

<SettingsTable section="comparison" />

## Feature tiles

Large clickable tiles with their own background color or image — good for routing shoppers to key categories or campaigns.
