These sections tell your brand story. Most of them share the same heading controls — **Eyebrow**, **Heading**, **Intro**, **Heading alignment** and an optional link — so a page reads consistently.

## Rich text

Copy-led sections built from **Eyebrow**, **Heading**, **Text** and **Button** blocks. The **Split** layout puts the heading on the left and flows the text into columns.

The **Statement (one large sentence)** layout sets the heading blocks as one large display sentence. With **Highlight notes** on, each italic phrase in the heading sits on the next of the color scheme's three strip colors, in turn. Statement is the layout for new Rich text sections.

## Image with text

An image beside your copy, or a card that overlaps the image. Add a **List** block for ingredients, steps or benefits.

**Note strip on the seam** draws a strip where the image meets the copy, in the side by side layout on larger screens. Pick a **Strip product** to use that product's notes; without one, the strip uses the color scheme's strip colors.

## Multicolumn

One section, several styles: icon columns, numbered steps, cards, a spec band, a timeline, or offers with prices. Each **Column** block is one item. **Step numbers** can be *01, 02, 03*, roman numerals or plain *1, 2, 3*.

## Collage

A grid of images, products and collections with one large item. **Darken image for light text** is on by default so the overlaid copy stays readable on any photo — turn it off per item if your photo's already dark enough on its own.

Choose a **Style**:

- **Editorial grid** — one to five items, including note cards. Product items carry their note strip down the left edge.
- **Tiles** — three items: one large item beside two stacked ones, with the label and title over the image. Note cards are skipped.

A **Note card** block is a block of color taken from an entry in your **Note** metaobject. Enter the entry's handle in **Note entry handle**; its color, name and summary fill the card. **Stage** sets the card's label, and its color when the note has none. Turn on **Light text** for dark note colors.

## Testimonials

Customer quotes as cards or one large quote at a time. Link a quote to the product it's about.

The **Large quote with note strips** layout shows the first quote large and stacks the others beside it. Each quote carries its product's note strip down its edge and a line with the customer's name, the product and the **Room** — where the scent was used, such as *Kitchen*.

<img src="/images/docs/testimonials.png" alt="The Testimonials section: one quote at a time with a star rating and pager." width="1353" height="425" />

## Logo list

Press logos or partner marks, with an optional press quote. A logo can link to the article.

Choose a **Layout**: **Single ruled row, heading at left** or **Centered, stacked**. **Monochrome logos** shows every logo in grayscale so mixed brand colors sit together quietly.

## Comparison table

Compare your product with up to three alternatives, row by row. **Tint column 1** turns the highlight on. Choose which column to highlight with **Highlighted column**, and how with **Highlight style**: a **Band** or a **Tint**.

## Feature tiles

Large clickable tiles with their own background color or image — good for routing shoppers to key categories or campaigns.

Choose a **Layout**:

- **Grid, sized by importance** — set each tile's **Width** to one, two or three columns out of six. With **Large first tile** on, the first tile spans two rows with a display-size title.
- **Rail of equal tiles** — every tile the same size.
