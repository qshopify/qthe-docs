## FAQ

Questions and answers as an accordion. The **Help center** layout adds search and groups questions by **Topic**. Answers are added to your page's structured data so search engines can show them.

The **Topic index beside questions** layout puts the heading and a list of topics on the left and the questions on the right. The first topic starts selected, and choosing another topic shows its questions. A question without a topic shows under every topic; when no question has a topic, the index is hidden and every question shows. This is the layout for new FAQ sections.

## Newsletter

A signup form that adds subscribers to your Shopify customer list with email marketing consent. Only one signup form shows per page — when this section is on a page, the footer's signup form hides itself.

The **Inline on a heavy rule** layout sets the heading and text on the left and an underlined email field with a text button on the right, over a heavy rule. It's the layout for new Newsletter sections.

## Countdown

Count down to a real date and time — a launch, the end of a sale, a holiday shipping cut-off. Set **Ends at** in your store's time zone and choose what happens **When the countdown ends**.

Choose a **Layout**:

- **Band with elapsed-time strip** — days, hours and minutes as large numbers over a note strip that fills toward its tip as time passes, from **Starts at** to **Ends at**. Enter **Starts at** in the same format as **Ends at**, or leave it blank to hide the strip. Pick a **Strip color from product** to use that product's base note color; without one, the strip uses the color scheme's third strip color.
- **Boxed units** — days, hours, minutes and seconds in separate boxes.

<Callout type="warning" title="Only for real deadlines">
Don't use a countdown for a deadline that resets or doesn't exist. Fake urgency misleads shoppers and breaks Shopify's rules.
</Callout>
