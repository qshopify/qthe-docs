## Hero

The hero is the most flexible first section. Choose a **Layout**:

- **Split — text beside image** with optional stats and a floating product card.
- **Full-bleed — text over image** with an optional background video.
- **Full-bleed — text in a panel** for busy images where text needs a calm background.
- **Statement — type only** for a bold line of copy.

Set a separate **Mobile image** when your desktop image is very wide. For background video, upload an MP4 in **Content > Files** and keep it short and silent — shoppers can pause it with the pause button.

## Slideshow

Up to several slides, each with its own image, copy and button. Autoplay always includes a pause button, and the slideshow stops on hover and focus.

Pick a **Product** on a slide to draw that slide's indicator as the product's [note strip](/docs/products/note-strip). The product also fills in the slide's image and button link when you leave those empty.

## Image banner

A single wide image with a caption — a quiet break between sections. **Full width** runs the image edge to edge; turn it off to keep it inside the page width.

Add **Eyebrow**, **Heading**, **Text** and **Button** blocks to show a solid text panel docked to a corner of the image. Choose the corner with **Text panel position** and its colors with **Text panel color scheme**. On phones the panel sits under the image. Without blocks, the section shows the image and its caption only.

## Page header

A title band for pages, collections and blogs, with an optional image and a product or article count. Every part falls back to the page being viewed, so an empty section still shows the right title.

Choose a **Style**:

- **Plain** — a large title with the intro, date or count beside it on one line, closed by a rule. This style has no image.
- **Editorial** — title and intro, small print at the foot and an image column on the right.

**Show date** shows when a page was published, on pages that have no **Small print**.

## Video

Play a Shopify-hosted video or a YouTube or Vimeo link. The player only loads when a shopper presses play, which keeps the page fast.

**Play button** places either a labelled button at the bottom left, showing your **Play button label (screen readers)**, or a round button in the center. Under **Chapters**, enter up to three **Chapter labels** separated by commas to draw a bar along the bottom of the video in the note strip colors. Pick a **Chapter colors from product** to use that product's top, heart and base note colors; without one, the color scheme's strip colors are used. Leave **Chapter labels** blank to hide the bar.
