## Announcement bar

Use the announcement bar for one to three short messages — a shipping threshold, a launch, a store event. Add one **Message** block per message.

- **All messages in one row** shows them side by side on desktop.
- **Rotate messages** shows one at a time, with optional arrows and a counter.

Keep each message under about 60 characters so it fits on one line on phones.

## Top bar

The top bar is a slim strip above the header with a message on one side and social media icons, a currency selector and a language selector on the other. Add it from the **Header** group with **Add section**.
