## 1.1.0 — Drydown

QThe now reads every product the way it unfolds. A [note strip](/docs/products/note-strip), one band per stage with the longest-lasting note at the tip, runs through the whole theme and is drawn from the product's own data.

### The note strip

- Strips draw from product metafields — top, heart and base notes, optionally linked to **Note** entries with a color and family — and fall back to the product's color swatches, then to three strip colors set in each color scheme. Band lengths come from a product metafield or a theme setting.
- Product cards carry the strip down the left edge or across the top, with a square quick add button on the image.
- New **Note tiers** product page layout: the page becomes the product's strip, with the buy box in the first tier and one tier per heart and base note, each with its own color scheme.
- New **Note wall** section: every product of a collection as a strip, in columns (a home page hero) or rows (a ledger with price and quick add), with family filters.
- Strips in the cart drawer, cart page, cart notification, quick view, predictive search, recommendations, recently viewed, bundle builder, testimonials, shoppable image hotspots, slideshow progress, video chapters, countdown, gift card, 404 and password pages, and a note rule above the footer.

### Store-wide

- New **Shapes and sizes** settings: strip tip, strip widths, button shape and case, heading size.
- New **Note strips** settings: default band lengths and the strip on product cards.
- Five redesigned color schemes, each with three strip colors.
- Three presets with their own templates: QThe, Seventh Steep and Vesper.
- New layouts on existing sections: collage, feature tiles, image banner, image with text, rich text, testimonials, FAQ, featured blog, newsletter, collection banner, collection list, comparison, main collection, main cart, blog, article, account and search. Every previous layout is still available.

### Fixes

- **Image with text**: with **Note strip on the seam** on and the image on the left, the text could land in the narrow strip column on wide screens. The image, strip and text now always sit in that order.
- **Header**: a long store name used as a text logo no longer runs into the header icons on phones. The letter spacing tightens below tablet width.
- **Collection list, Collection tabs, Collection banner and filter chips** now use a collection's own `custom.color` metafield before the family color.
- **Note wall**: in the Columns layout, a long product name no longer runs into the strip above it.
- **Collage**: a newly added note card shows the first entry of your note library until you pick one.
- **Note pages**: a note without an image now shows a full swatch of its own color instead of a placeholder illustration.
- **Quick view**: the image now fills the full height of the modal when the buy box is taller than the photo.
- **Collection tiles**: text on image tiles (All collections, Collection list, Collage) sits on a darker overlay, so it stays readable on light photos.
- American English throughout the theme's default content.

<Callout type="note" title="Updating from 1.0.0">
Your settings, sections and content carry over. Several sections have new default layouts, and a setting you never changed can pick up its new default, so preview your pages in the theme editor before you publish the update. Every 1.0.0 layout is still available. To use the note strip with your own colors, add the notes metafields described in [Product details from metafields and tags](/docs/products/product-data).
</Callout>

## 1.0.0

The first release of QThe.

### Shopping

- Product page built from individual blocks: vendor, title, price, rating, variant picker with swatches, buy buttons, inventory, pickup availability, delivery estimate, trust badges, collapsible tabs, share, custom liquid and app blocks.
- Accelerated checkout and Shop Pay Installments on the product page, featured product and quick view.
- Subscriptions, pre-orders and gift cards with recipient emails.
- Images, Shopify-hosted video, YouTube, Vimeo and 3D models in the gallery, featured product and quick view.
- Quick view and quick add from product cards.
- Cart drawer, cart page or cart notification, with order notes, discounts, unit prices and a free shipping goal.
- Filtering, sorting and predictive search. Related and complementary recommendations, and recently viewed products.

### Content

- Heroes: hero, slideshow, image banner, page header and video.
- Storytelling: rich text, image with text, multicolumn, collage, testimonials, logo list, comparison, feature tiles, FAQ, countdown, newsletter and custom liquid.
- Commerce: featured product, featured collection, collection tabs, collection list, shoppable image, bundle builder, gift card purchase and blog posts.
- A scent finder quiz and a note library.

### Store-wide

- Two header layouts with mega navigation, an announcement bar and a top bar.
- Three footer layouts with signup, footer menus, social media icons, Follow on Shop, payment icons and country and language selectors.
- Shopify's customer account component in the header.
- Newsletter popup, cookie banner and info drawer.
- Five color schemes and font pickers for headings and body text.
