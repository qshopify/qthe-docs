Add these sections to the **Footer** group so they work on every page.

## Newsletter popup

Opens after a delay, or when the pointer leaves the page on desktop. Once a shopper closes it or signs up, it stays closed for the rest of their visit, and for the number of days in **Keep closed after dismissal**. Set it to 0 to show the popup again in the next visit. After signing up you can show a thank-you message with a discount code — create the code in **Discounts** first.

Choose a **Layout**: **Docked card** is a small card in the bottom-left corner that leaves the page usable. **Centered modal** opens in the middle of the page over a scrim.

## Cookie banner

QThe works with Shopify's own cookie banner. If you turn on the banner in **Settings > Customer privacy**, QThe's banner stays hidden. If you prefer QThe's design, leave Shopify's banner off and add this section — it only appears to visitors in regions that require consent, and every choice is saved through Shopify's Customer Privacy API, so analytics and marketing apps respect it.

<img src="/images/docs/cookie-banner.png" alt="The cookie consent banner as a card in the bottom-right corner, with Manage, Reject non-essential and Accept all." width="1385" height="900" />

Choose a **Layout**: **Card** is a small card in the bottom-right corner with a *Manage* link and two equal buttons to reject or accept. **Bar** runs along the bottom of the page.

## Info drawer

A side panel with notes, a table or text — a size guide, an ingredient list, care instructions. Open it from its own trigger button. Developers can open it from any element with the attribute `data-open="#drawer-ID"`, where *ID* is the **Drawer ID**.

<img src="/images/docs/info-drawer.png" alt="An info drawer open from its trigger button, sliding in from the side." width="1385" height="900" />

Add **Link row** blocks for links such as a safety data sheet. Turn on **Open in a new tab** for files and outside pages.
