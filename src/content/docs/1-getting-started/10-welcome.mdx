QThe is an editorial Shopify theme for brands with a curated catalog — home fragrance, beauty, candles, skincare and lifestyle goods. It pairs large, quiet imagery with precise typography, and puts the details shoppers care about (notes, ingredients, sizes, refills) right next to the buy button.

## What you get

- **A complete store out of the box** — home page, collection, product, cart, search, blog, article, contact, FAQ and account pages, all built from sections you can rearrange.
- **More than 40 sections** — heroes, storytelling blocks, commerce sections such as a bundle builder and shoppable image, and a scent finder quiz.
- **Built-in Shopify features** — filtering, predictive search, pickup availability, subscriptions, gift cards, accelerated checkout, Shop Pay Installments, Follow on Shop and the customer account component.
- **Accessible by default** — keyboard navigation, visible focus, skip link and WCAG AA color contrast on every preset color scheme.
