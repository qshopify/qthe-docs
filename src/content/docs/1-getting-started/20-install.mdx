## Add the theme to your store

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store > Themes**.
2. Open the QThe listing in the Shopify Theme Store and click **Try theme** to preview it, or **Add theme** after purchase.
3. QThe appears in your **Theme library**. It is not live until you publish it.

<img src="/images/docs/theme-library.png" alt="QThe theme card in the Theme library, with Customize and the ··· menu open." width="1226" height="983" />

## Customize before you publish

Click **Customize** next to QThe in your theme library. The theme editor opens with your real products, collections and menus, so you can see exactly how your store will look.

A good first pass:

1. **Logo and colors** — open **Theme settings** (the paintbrush icon) and set your logo, favicon, color schemes and fonts. See [Theme settings](/docs/getting-started/theme-settings).
2. **Navigation** — QThe's header uses your **Main menu** and the footer uses your **Footer menu** from **Online Store > Navigation**.
3. **Home page** — replace the sample sections' images and copy, and remove any section you don't need.
4. **Product page** — open a product in the editor and arrange the product information blocks. See [Product page](/docs/products/product-page).

## Publish

When you're happy, go back to **Online Store > Themes**, click **⋯** next to QThe and choose **Publish**. Your previous theme stays in the library, so you can switch back at any time.

<Callout type="warning" title="Duplicate before you edit code">
If you or a developer plan to edit theme code, always duplicate the theme first (**⋯ > Duplicate**) and make changes on the copy. See [Custom code](/docs/advanced/custom-code).

<img src="/images/docs/duplicate-theme.png" alt="Hovering Duplicate in the theme's ⋯ menu." width="1226" height="983" />
</Callout>

## Install from a file

If you received QThe as a `.zip` file, go to **Online Store > Themes**, click **Add theme > Upload zip file**, and choose the file. Don't unzip it first.
