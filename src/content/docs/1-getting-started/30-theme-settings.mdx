Theme settings apply to every page. Open them from the theme editor by clicking the **paintbrush icon** in the left sidebar.

## Presets

QThe comes in three presets. Each one is a full set of theme settings plus its own home, collection and product templates. Choose a preset when you add the theme from the Theme Store; after that, every setting below can be changed.

| | QThe | Seventh Steep | Vesper |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Palette | Dark brown text on pale green-gray, with sage, brown and amber strips | Pale text on dark green, with saffron buttons and strips | Plum text on blush, with terracotta buttons |
| Heading font | Unbounded | Instrument Serif | Figtree, extra bold |
| Body font | Figtree | DM Sans | Figtree |
| **Strip tip** | Pointed | Long point | Flat |
| **Strip width on cards** / **on product pages** | 14 px / 28 px | 10 px / 20 px | 12 px / 24 px |
| **Button shape** | Square | Square | Pill |
| **Heading size** | 100% | 110% | 95% |
| **Default band lengths** | 22,33,45 | 40,40,20 | 22,33,45 |
| **Strip on product cards** | Left edge | Top edge | Top edge |
| Home page opens with | **Note wall** in columns | **Note wall** in rows, a ledger with price and quick add | **Featured product** in the Tier layout |

All three use **Sentence case** buttons and the **Note tiers** product page layout. Every other setting — page width, product cards, cart — is the same in each preset.

## Colors

QThe uses **color schemes**: named sets of background, text, border, button and accent colors. Every section has a **Color scheme** setting, so you can alternate light and dark bands down a page while keeping every color pairing accessible.

Each scheme also has three strip colors: **Strip — top**, **Strip — heart** and **Strip — base**. They color a [note strip](/docs/products/note-strip) when a product has no note colors and no color swatches, and they color the strips that aren't tied to a product, such as the 404 page and the countdown. Strip colors change per scheme, so a strip on a dark section can use lighter tones than one on a light section.

<Callout type="tip" title="Keep text readable">
When you change a scheme, check the contrast between **Background** and **Text**, and between **Button** and **Button label**. Aim for a ratio of at least 4.5:1 for body text.
</Callout>

## Typography

Choose one font for headings and one for body text from Shopify's font library. Bold and italic styles load automatically.

The defaults are **Unbounded** (light) for headings and **Figtree** for body text. The Seventh Steep and Vesper presets use their own pairs — see [Presets](#presets). Use **Heading size** under **Shapes and sizes** to scale every heading at once.

## Shapes and sizes

Shapes and sizes change the feel of every page at once: the shape of the note strip, the shape and case of buttons, and the size of headings. The note strip stays visible in every combination.

- **Strip tip** — how the longest-lasting end of every strip finishes: **Pointed**, **Long point** or **Flat**.
- **Strip width on cards** — the thickness of the strip along product cards, from 6 to 24 px.
- **Strip width on product pages** — the thickness of the rail that runs down the product page in the **Note tiers** layout, from 12 to 40 px.
- **Button shape** — **Square**, **Soft corners** or **Pill**.
- **Button text** — **Sentence case** or **Uppercase**.
- **Heading size** — scales every heading from 80% to 120% of the default.

## Note strips

These settings control the [note strip](/docs/products/note-strip) across the store.

- **Default band lengths** — the relative length of the top, heart and base bands, as three numbers separated by commas. The default `22,33,45` makes the base band, which lasts longest, the longest. A product's `custom.note_profile` metafield overrides this for that product.
- **Strip on product cards** — draw the strip down the **Left edge** of each card, across the **Top edge**, or choose **None** to hide it on cards. Strips in the cart, search and other sections aren't affected.
