Theme settings apply to every page. Open them from the theme editor by clicking the **paintbrush icon** in the left sidebar.

## Logo and favicon

Your logo is set in the **Header** section, so you can size it per layout. The favicon — the small icon in the browser tab — is set here.

<SettingsTable global="Logo and favicon" />

## Colors

QThe uses **color schemes**: named sets of background, text, border, button and accent colors. Every section has a **Color scheme** setting, so you can alternate light and dark bands down a page while keeping every color pairing accessible.

<SettingsTable global="Colors" />

<Callout type="tip" title="Keep text readable">
When you change a scheme, check the contrast between **Background** and **Text**, and between **Button** and **Button label**. Aim for a ratio of at least 4.5:1 for body text.
</Callout>
