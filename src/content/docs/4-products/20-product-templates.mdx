QThe ships several product templates. Assign one in your admin: open a product, find **Theme template** in the right column, choose the template and save.

| Template | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Default product** | The standard layout with recommendations. |
| **showcase** | A larger gallery with storytelling sections underneath — use it for a signature product. |
| **subscription** | Shows the product's selling plans and a *how it works* band. The product needs a subscription app that creates selling plans. |
| **preorder** | Relabels the buy button and adds a shipping note under it. |
| **bundle** | Shows what's inside the set. |
| **gift-card** | Amount buttons and the gift card recipient form. Use it for your gift card product. |

## Subscriptions

When a product has selling plans, the buy box shows a one-time / subscribe choice and the plan's price. **Pre-select the first subscription plan** in Theme settings makes subscribing the default. The chosen plan appears on the cart line.

## Gift cards

Create a gift card product in **Products > Gift cards**, assign the **gift-card** template, and add the **Gift card recipient** block if you want shoppers to send it to someone. After purchase, the recipient receives an email with a link to your store's gift card page, which shows the code, a QR code and an *Add to Apple Wallet* button.
