The product page is built from the **Product information** section. Everything in the buy box — title, price, variant picker, buy buttons, description — is a separate block, so you can reorder, remove or repeat them.

## Gallery

All product media shows in the gallery: images, Shopify-hosted videos, YouTube and Vimeo videos and 3D models (with *View in your space* on supported phones). When a shopper picks a variant that has its own image, the gallery jumps to it.

Choose a **Desktop gallery** layout and a **Media shape**. Images of different shapes are fitted into the same frame, so a mixed set never breaks the layout. Turn on **Sticky add-to-cart bar** to keep the buy button in reach while shoppers scroll; it stops at the footer.

## Buy box blocks

- **Title** and **Price** — the price shows compare-at prices, unit prices and a *tax included* note when your store shows prices with tax.
- **Variant picker** — buttons, swatches (from your product's color swatches) or dropdowns. Sold-out combinations stay visible but are marked.
- **Buy buttons** — quantity, add to cart, accelerated checkout buttons (on by default), and Shop Pay Installments messaging. Use **Button label override** for pre-orders.
- **Pickup availability** — shows whether the selected variant can be picked up at your locations, with a link to all stores. Requires local pickup in **Settings > Shipping and delivery**.
- **Delivery estimate** — an estimated delivery window from your own cut-off time and business days.
- **Inventory status** — shows a low-stock message when the real inventory is at or under your **Low stock threshold**.
- **Gift card recipient** — lets shoppers send a gift card to someone else by email, with a message and send date.
- **Scent notes**, **Collapsible tab**, **Delivery and perks**, **Pairs with**, **What's inside**, **Trust badges**, **Share** — supporting details. Each one hides itself when it has nothing to show.
- **Custom liquid** and **app blocks** — add review stars, size charts or other app features exactly where you want them.
