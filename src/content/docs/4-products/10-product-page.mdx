The product page is built from the **Product information** section. Everything in the buy box — title, price, variant picker, buy buttons, description — is a separate block, so you can reorder, remove or repeat them.

## Layouts

Choose a **Layout** in the **Product information** section:

- **Classic** — the gallery beside the buy box.
- **Note tiers** — the page becomes the product's [note strip](/docs/products/note-strip), read from top to bottom. The default product template and all three presets use this layout.

### Note tiers layout

In the **Note tiers** layout, the page is split into up to three tiers, one per stage of the product's notes. A rail down the left side of each tier takes the color of that note, and the last tier's rail ends in the strip's tip. Set the rail's thickness with **Strip width on product pages** in **Theme settings > Theme style**.

- **Top tier** — every block before the first **Tier** block forms the buy box, shown beside the gallery.
- **Heart and base tiers** — each **Tier** block starts a new tier. The tier opens with its label (*Heart* or *Base*), the note's name and, when **Show note summary** is on, the note's summary. Every block after a **Tier** block sits inside that tier, until the next **Tier** block.

Add up to two **Tier** blocks. Each one has its own settings:

- **Note** — **Heart** or **Base**: which of the product's notes the tier shows.
- **Show note summary** — shows the `summary` field of the note's entry in your **Note** metaobject.
- **Color scheme** — the tier's own colors, so the tiers can step from light to dark down the page.

The note's name and summary come from the product's `custom.notes_heart` or `custom.notes_base` metafield and its **Note** entry. See [Product details from metafields and tags](/docs/products/product-data#notes-metafields).

<Callout type="tip" title="Move blocks between tiers">
Drag a block above or below a **Tier** block in the theme editor to move it into another tier. In the default product template, the title, price, variant picker and buy buttons sit in the top tier, *How it performs*, *How to use* and **Pairs with** in the heart tier, and the shipping and safety tabs and **Scent notes** in the base tier.
</Callout>

In the **Classic** layout, **Tier** blocks are ignored, so you can switch layouts without removing them.

## Gallery

All product media shows in the gallery: images, Shopify-hosted videos, YouTube and Vimeo videos and 3D models (with *View in your space* on supported phones). When a shopper picks a variant that has its own image, the gallery jumps to it.

Choose a **Desktop gallery** layout and a **Media shape**. Images of different shapes are fitted into the same frame, so a mixed set never breaks the layout. Turn on **Sticky add-to-cart bar** to keep the buy button in reach while shoppers scroll; it stops at the footer.

## Buy box blocks

- **Title** and **Price** — the price shows compare-at prices, unit prices and a *tax included* note when your store shows prices with tax.
- **Variant picker** — buttons, swatches (from your product's color swatches) or dropdowns. Sold-out combinations stay visible but are marked.
- **Buy buttons** — quantity, add to cart, accelerated checkout buttons (on by default), and Shop Pay Installments messaging. Use **Button label override** for pre-orders.
- **Pickup availability** — shows whether the selected variant can be picked up at your locations, with a link to all stores. Requires local pickup in **Settings > Shipping and delivery**.
- **Delivery estimate** — an estimated delivery window from your own cut-off time and business days.
- **Inventory status** — shows a low-stock message when the real inventory is at or under your **Low stock threshold**.
- **Gift card recipient** — lets shoppers send a gift card to someone else by email, with a message and send date.
- **Scent notes**, **Collapsible tab**, **Delivery and perks**, **Pairs with**, **What's inside**, **Trust badges**, **Share** — supporting details. Each one hides itself when it has nothing to show.
- **Custom liquid** and **app blocks** — add review stars, size charts or other app features exactly where you want them.
