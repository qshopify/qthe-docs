## Product recommendations

Add the **Product recommendations** section below the product information.

- **Related products** are chosen automatically by Shopify from purchase and browsing patterns.
- **Complementary products** are the ones you pick in the **Search & Discovery** app — for example a refill for a diffuser.

Add the section twice to show both.

## Quick view and quick add

Turn these on in **Theme settings > Product cards**.

- **Quick add** puts an add button on each card. Products with one variant go straight to the cart; products with options open quick view, or the product page when quick view is off.
- **Quick view** opens a window with the gallery (including video and 3D), price, variant picker, buy button and Shop Pay Installments messaging, so shoppers can buy without leaving the collection.
