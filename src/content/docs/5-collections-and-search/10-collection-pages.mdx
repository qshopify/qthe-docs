A collection page has two main sections: the **Collection banner** at the top and the **Product grid** below it.

## Collection banner

Shows the collection title, description and image. Choose a **Layout**:

- **Band** — a block of the collection's family color beside a large title, with the description and product count at the far edge of the band. The color is **Band color**; when that's blank, it's the collection's `custom.color` metafield, then the color of the **Note** entries whose family matches the collection title, or else the color scheme's strip color. Band is the layout for new Collection banner sections.
- **Classic** — breadcrumbs, a large title and description, and **Collection pills navigation**: a row of links beside the heading — moods, families or sub-collections — with the current one highlighted.

## Product grid

- **Filtering** uses Shopify's own filters. Choose which filters appear (availability, price, product type, vendor, variant options, metafields) in the **Search & Discovery** app.
- **Sorting** offers Shopify's sort orders: featured, best selling, alphabetical, price and date.
- **Pagination** can be page numbers or a *Load more* button.
- Cards show sale badges, price ranges for products with several prices, and unit prices.

Add **Promo tile** blocks to place a campaign tile inside the grid, or **Sidebar note** and **Sidebar image** blocks under the filters.

### Bottles and strips views

**Show Bottles / Strips toggle** lets shoppers switch the grid between the usual product cards (**Bottles**) and **Strips**, a board of tall [note strips](/docs/products/note-strip) with product names. Set the view the page opens in with **Default view**. Filtering, sorting and *Load more* keep the chosen view.

### Family chips

**Show family chips** shows one filter as a row of chips above the grid, each with a tick in its family color. Enter the filter's name in **Chip filter** — for example *Family* — or leave it blank to use the first list filter. A chip's color is the filter value's swatch, or else the color of the **Note** entries in that family. Chips need filtering to be on.

## All collections page

The page at `/collections` lists every collection as a grid or list. Collections without an image use their first product's image.
