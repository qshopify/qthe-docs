## What happens after add to cart

In **Theme settings > Cart**, set **After adding to cart**:

- **Open the cart drawer** — a panel slides in with the bag, a free shipping meter and upsells.
- **Go to the cart page** — classic behavior.
- **Show a notification** — a small confirmation near the header, so shoppers keep browsing.

Every add to cart shows a loading state on the button and a confirmation, so shoppers always know it worked.

## Free shipping goal

Set **Free shipping threshold** in Theme settings (in your store's currency). The drawer shows how much is left and celebrates when the goal is reached. Add a **Second milestone** in the cart drawer section — for example a free sample at a higher amount.

In the cart drawer, **Free shipping progress** draws the meter as a **Strip** — a note strip filling toward its tip — or a classic **Bar**.

<Callout type="note" title="The meter doesn't change shipping rates">
The meter is a message. Set up the matching free shipping rate in **Settings > Shipping and delivery**.
</Callout>

## Cart page

The cart page lists each item with its image, options, selling plan, discounts and unit price, plus quantity controls. It supports order notes, discount codes, automatic discounts and accelerated checkout buttons.

- **Show note strips** draws each line's note strip.
- **Show strips across a day** draws the strips of the products in the cart side by side, each read from top to base note. It shows when the cart holds two or more different products.

## Cart notification

Choose a **Style**: **Strip toast** is a small notice with the product's note strip, its image and name and a link to the cart. **Classic** shows the line just added, with *View bag*, checkout and *Continue shopping*.
