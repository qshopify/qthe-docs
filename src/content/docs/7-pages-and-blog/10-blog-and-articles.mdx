## Blog layouts

Pick a layout in the **Blog posts** section, or assign one of the alternate blog templates:

- **Grid** — cards with an optional featured latest article.
- **Index list (grouped by month)** — a compact archive for frequent publishers.
- **Grid with sidebar** — tags and a newsletter signup beside the grid.
- **Rows** — one ruled row per article: image, tag and reading time, title, and the product the article is about, marked with a tick in its heart note color.

Cards use each article's image, title and excerpt. Write a short excerpt in the article editor; without one, the start of the article is used.

To link an article to a product, fill the article's `custom.product` metafield with a product or a list of products. See [Collection and article metafields](/docs/products/product-data#collection-and-article-metafields).

## Articles

Articles show the title, publish date, author and featured image. **Show "In this story" contents** builds a table of contents from the article's subheadings. Comments appear when you allow them on the blog, and are paginated.

- **Show note strip rail** runs a note strip beside the reading column — the strip of the first product in the article's `custom.product` metafield, or the color scheme's strip colors.
- **Show oils in this article** adds an *In this article* list of the products in `custom.product`. Without the metafield, the list is hidden.
