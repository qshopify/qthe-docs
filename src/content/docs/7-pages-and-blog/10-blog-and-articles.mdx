## Blog layouts

Pick a layout in the **Blog posts** section, or assign one of the alternate blog templates:

- **Grid** — cards with an optional featured latest article.
- **Index list (grouped by month)** — a compact archive for frequent publishers.
- **Grid with sidebar** — tags and a newsletter signup beside the grid.

Cards use each article's image, title and excerpt. Write a short excerpt in the article editor; without one, the start of the article is used.

## Articles

Articles show the title, publish date, author and featured image. **Show "In this story" contents** builds a table of contents from the article's subheadings. Comments appear when you allow them on the blog, and are paginated.
