The **Quiz** section walks shoppers through a few questions, one per screen, then shows a matching result with recommended products and a ready-made kit they can add to the cart in one step. It's built entirely from blocks, so you can change every question without code.

<img src="/images/docs/scent-finder-quiz.png" alt="A Quiz question screen: a mood photo on the left and the question, four answer cards and Back/Continue buttons on the right." width="1425" height="820" />

## Set it up

1. Create a page — for example *Find your scent* — and assign the **quiz** page template, or add the **Quiz** section to any page.
2. Add one **Question** block per step. Each question has up to four answers.
3. Give each answer a **Match tag** — a short word such as *calm*, *fresh*, *warm* or *night*.
4. Add one **Result** block per outcome. In **Match tags**, list the tags that lead to it, separated by commas, and pick its **Recommended products**.
5. Optional: choose a **Starter product** for the kit, and an **adds product** for answers that should add a specific item.

## How results are chosen

When the shopper finishes, the quiz shows the first result whose tags match one of the chosen answers. Order your result blocks from most specific to most general, and keep a catch-all result last.
