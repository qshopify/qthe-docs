## Pages

Every page in **Online Store > Pages** uses the **Page** section. Add a **Sidebar navigation** menu to link related pages — for example shipping, returns, FAQ and contact — and an optional *last updated* date for policies.

## Contact page

Assign the **contact** page template to your contact page. Messages arrive at your store's sender email. Add **Topics** to give shoppers a dropdown of reasons, and turn on order number or phone fields if you need them.

<img src="/images/docs/main-contact.png" alt="The Contact page: an info grid on the left and the contact form on the right." width="1440" height="1000" />

<SettingsTable section="main-contact" />

## FAQ page

Assign the **faq** template to a page to get a full help center: search, topics and an accordion of questions. See the [FAQ section](/docs/sections/engagement#faq) for its settings.

## Customer account page

**Show next refill** puts the customer's latest subscription purchase first on the account page, with its note strip, plan and the extra link to manage it — for example a link to your subscription app's portal.

<SettingsTable section="main-account" />

## 404 page

Choose a **Layout**: **Strip** shows a note strip that stops short, in the color scheme's strip colors, beside the message, a search field and a link back. **Numeral** shows the message beside a panel with a large *404*, or with your **Image** when you add one.

<SettingsTable section="main-404" />

## Password page

While your store is password protected, the password page shows your brand, a message, a signup form and the password form. The **Outlined strips** layout draws your products' strips as outlines, waiting for opening day — one per product of the **Strips collection**, up to 12, or **Strips without a collection** outlines when no collection is set. **Centered** is a classic centered layout.
